# Oasis Infobyte Internship — Retail Sales Data Analysis

## Project Overview
This project performs Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on retail sales data to identify sales patterns, product performance, and business insights.

## Tools and Technologies
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Google Colab

## Analysis Performed
- Dataset inspection and missing-value analysis
- Descriptive statistics
- Sales by product category
- Top 10 products by sales
- Monthly and quarterly sales trends
- Correlation heatmap
- Profit and regional sales analysis

## Key Findings
- Technology had the highest total sales among the product categories.
- Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier ranked highest among the top 10 products by sales.
- Monthly sales varied over time.
- Customer age and gender were unavailable in the selected dataset.

## Business Recommendations
1. Review demand and inventory for popular technology products.
2. Investigate product costs, pricing, and discounts to improve profitability.
3. Use sales trends to plan inventory and promotional campaigns.

## Conclusion
This project demonstrates how exploratory data analysis can reveal retail sales patterns and support data-driven business decisions.
